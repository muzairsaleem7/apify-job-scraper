Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Job Scraper is a personal tool that runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The developer never receives your data.

Data the app uses

  • Job listings from public job boards and from Apify, using your own Apify API token. They are saved on your computer in the app's output and data folders.
  • Google account data, only if you click “Connect Google”:
    • your email address, to show which account is connected;
    • access to the Google Drive files the app creates (scope drive.file): one folder, one spreadsheet per run, and a Job Tracker spreadsheet. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

How Google data is used and stored

  • The app uses Google access only to create those spreadsheets, write job listings into them, and read the Job Tracker's “Job key” column so it doesn't add the same job twice.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer (data/google.json). It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Job Scraper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Click “Disconnect” in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the spreadsheets and the “Job Scraper” folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete the app's output and data folders on your computer.
